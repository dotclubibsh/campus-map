      Campus Connect

      The IFHE Hyderabad campus map, made by Dot Club, IBS Hyderabad

      Who we are

      We're Dot Club, the techno-managerial club of IBS Hyderabad. The campus is big, a lot of the blocks look alike, and asking a senior where LT-K is gets old fast. So we built a map.

      What it does

      Search any of the places on campus, from lecture theatres and faculty cabins to the laundry and the clinic. Tap one and the map shows you where it is. Tap Directions and it walks you there.

      What's inside

      • Search and categoriesType a name, or browse food, hostels, academics and sports.
      • 2D and 3D viewsFlat for finding your way, 3D for spotting the building.
      • Walking directionsTurn by turn, read out loud, with a step-free option.
      • Live location and compassShows where you are and which way you face. Your location stays on your phone.
      • QR start pointsScan a code on a wall to start directions from that spot, even indoors.
      • Pin and share a spotLong-press anywhere to drop a pin, or open a place, then tap Share to send it. Friends get the exact spot with directions.
      • Works offlineOpen it once and it keeps working without internet.

      To install on iPhone, tap Share and then Add to Home Screen.
