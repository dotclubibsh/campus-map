Privacy notice

This notice explains what Campus Connect, the IFHE Hyderabad campus map, collects and why, as the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act) asks us to. Dot Club, the techno-managerial club of IBS Hyderabad, runs the map and decides how it handles data. Under the Act, that makes us the "data fiduciary".

There are no accounts, no sign-up forms and no passwords. You can search, browse and get directions without telling us who you are.

What we collect

  • Nothing, unless you agree to analytics.
  • If you agree, Google Analytics records which pages you open, roughly where in the world you are (from your IP address), your device and browser type, and anonymous usage events. We use this to see which places people look for and where the map lets them down.

What we never collect

  • Your name, phone number, email or college ID.
  • Your GPS location. See the next section.
  • Anything you type into search. It stays in your browser.

Your location

When you tap directions and allow location, your phone works out where you are and the map uses that to draw your route. The calculation happens on your device. We do not send your position to our servers, to Google Analytics or to anyone else, and we do not store a history of where you have been.

The compass works the same way: your phone reads which way you are facing and the map turns the arrow. You can refuse location and still pick a starting point by hand or scan a QR code.

You can turn location off at any time in your browser or phone settings.

Consent

On your first visit we ask one question: may we use analytics? We ask before anything is loaded. Saying no changes nothing about how the map works.

Your answer is saved in your browser, so we do not ask again on every visit. You can change it here whenever you like, and withdrawing is as easy as agreeing was.

Analytics are currently not set.

If you turn analytics off, we also delete the Google Analytics cookies from this site in your browser.

We do not sell data and we do not share it with advertisers. Google Analytics is set up with ad personalisation and Google signals switched off.

Your rights under the DPDP Act

As a data principal you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and who we have shared it with;
  • correct, complete or erase it;
  • stop processing it, by withdrawing consent;
  • nominate someone to act for you if you are unable to.

Because we never ask who you are, we usually hold nothing we could link to you. The analytics we keep are pseudonymous: a random ID, not a name. If you want them gone, turn analytics off above and clear your browser data. That removes the ID, and nothing new is collected.

For any request or complaint, message us (see Contact). We aim to reply within 7 days. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

How long we keep data

Settings in your browser stay until you clear them. Analytics data is kept by Google for the period set in our Google Analytics account, which Google limits to between 2 and 14 months, and is then deleted automatically. We do not keep any other copies.

If you are under 18

The DPDP Act needs a parent or guardian's consent before anyone processes a child's personal data. The map works fully without analytics, so if you are under 18, please choose "No thanks" when asked, or ask a parent or guardian first.

Security

The map has no logins and no database, so there are no passwords or personal records to steal. We still take care with what is there:

  • the whole site is served over HTTPS;
  • a strict Content Security Policy stops the page running scripts from sources we have not approved;
  • third-party scripts are pinned to exact versions, and checked with integrity hashes where browsers support it;
  • place names are escaped before they are shown, so map data cannot inject code;
  • links to other sites open without giving them control of this tab.

Found a problem?

If you find a security issue, please tell us privately first (see Contact) and give us a reasonable time to fix it before sharing it. Please do not test against other people's devices or try to disrupt the site. We will thank you by name on this page if you would like.

Terms of use

Using the map means you accept these terms.

Directions are a guide

Paths were traced from aerial imagery and OpenStreetMap, and GPS on phones can be off by several metres. Gates may be locked, paths closed and buildings changed since the map was made. Always follow campus signs, security staff and common sense, and watch where you are going rather than the screen.

Step-free routes

"Avoid stairs" only avoids stairs we have marked, and lifts are not mapped yet. If you rely on step-free access, check with the campus office before relying on a route.

Fair use

Use the map for finding your way. Do not try to break it, overload it, or copy it wholesale to pass off as your own. The road data comes from OpenStreetMap and remains under its Open Database Licence.
